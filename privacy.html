Privacy policy

Updated November 25, 2026
Applies to Hushgrove for iOS, including Hushgrove Pro.

Information in the app

Hushgrove stores your task titles, focus-session durations and timestamps, session outcomes, optional usefulness ratings, saved focus routines, earned and spent points, island improvements, and preferences locally on your device. The app does not transmit these records to the developer.

Accounts and advertising

Hushgrove works without an account, ads, or cloud sync.

Optional usage analytics

Hushgrove asks for permission before sending usage data. If you choose to share it, Hushgrove sends app opens, screen views, selected button taps, and events such as completed focus sessions, completed builds, and confirmed subscriptions to Google Analytics for Firebase. Google uses an app installation identifier and technical information such as your app version, device model, and approximate region to produce usage reports. We use these reports to improve the app. This setup disables advertising identifiers and the use of app events for personalized ads. Task names, individual focus history, saved routines, and island records stay on your device. To withdraw consent, open Island → Settings → Data and turn off Share usage data. How Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Notifications and sound

With your permission, Hushgrove schedules local reminders for focus sessions. Notifications and Live Activities may show your task title and timer on the lock screen or Dynamic Island according to your iPhone settings. Ambient sound files are bundled with the app and play locally. Hushgrove does not record audio or request microphone access.

App Store subscriptions

Optional Hushgrove Pro subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. The app uses StoreKit to retrieve subscription options and verify your purchase status on your device. Apple processes purchases under its own privacy policy. Hushgrove does not receive your payment card details or send transaction records to a developer-operated server. Subscription access can be restored with the Apple Account used for the purchase; your session history and island do not sync between devices.

Retention and deletion

Session and island records remain on your device until you delete progress in the app or remove the app. Delete all progress removes local session history, saved routines, island improvements, and point records. Deleting local progress or the app does not cancel an App Store subscription; manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Preferences and iOS notification permissions are managed separately. Device backups may contain app data according to your Apple settings; Hushgrove does not manage those backups.

Individual analytics events are kept according to the Google Analytics retention settings for Hushgrove. You can contact us at dimadd611@gmail.com to request deletion of analytics data. We may ask for details to identify the data associated with your installation.

Support emails and this website

If you email dimadd611@gmail.com, your email address and message are processed to respond to your request. Email services may process those messages. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve your request; you can ask for its deletion using the same address. Visiting support or privacy pages opens a website outside the app. The hosting provider may process technical web-request information under its own privacy practices. These pages do not use developer-added analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts.

Your choices

You can edit preferences, disable reminders, change notification permissions in iPhone Settings, and delete local progress. Avoid putting sensitive information in task titles if you allow notification previews.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app’s privacy practices change. The effective date will be updated on this page. For questions or requests, contact dimadd611@gmail.com.

Lifetime gifts

Hushgrove Pro Lifetime is permanent Pro access received through an Apple offer code. Redeem a code or restore access from the Pro screen using the same Apple Account. Apple handles redemption and purchase records. A Lifetime gift does not cancel an existing subscription; manage subscriptions in your Apple Account. Island progress remains stored on your device.
