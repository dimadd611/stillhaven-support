Privacy policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026
Applies to Stillhaven for iOS, version 1.0.

Information in the app

Stillhaven stores your task titles, focus-session durations and timestamps, session outcomes, earned and spent points, island improvements, and preferences locally on your device. The app does not transmit these records to the developer.

No accounts or tracking

The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, third-party analytics, tracking, or developer-operated cloud sync.

Notifications and sound

With your permission, Stillhaven schedules local reminders for focus sessions. Notification content may include your task title and can appear on your lock screen according to your iPhone settings. Ambient sound files are bundled with the app and play locally. Stillhaven does not record audio or request microphone access.

Retention and deletion

Session and island records remain on your device until you delete progress in the app or remove the app. Delete all progress removes local session history, island improvements, and point records. Preferences and iOS notification permissions are managed separately. Device backups may contain app data according to your Apple settings; Stillhaven does not manage those backups.

Support emails and this website

If you email dimadd611@gmail.com, your email address and message are processed to respond to your request. Email services may process those messages. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve your request; you can ask for its deletion using the same address. Visiting support or privacy pages opens a website outside the app. The hosting provider may process technical web-request information under its own privacy practices. These pages do not use developer-added analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts.

Your choices

You can edit preferences, disable reminders, change notification permissions in iPhone Settings, and delete local progress. Avoid putting sensitive information in task titles if you allow notification previews.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app’s privacy practices change. The effective date will be updated on this page. For questions or requests, contact dimadd611@gmail.com.
