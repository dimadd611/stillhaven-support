A quiet place to focus.

Hushgrove is a focus timer that turns completed sessions into small improvements for your woodland island.

Need a hand?

Email dimadd611@gmail.com. Include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened. Please avoid sending private task titles or other sensitive information.

How do I earn points?

Complete a focus session to earn one point per planned minute. Paused sessions can be resumed. Cancelling a session does not earn points.

How do I build on my island?

Open Build to preview an improvement, see its cost and prerequisites, and build it when you have enough points. Building is unavailable while a session is unfinished.

Why did I not receive a reminder?

Enable session reminders in Hushgrove and allow notifications in iPhone Settings. Focus modes and your notification settings may silence reminders.

Where is my progress saved?

Tasks, session history, and island progress are stored locally on your iPhone. Hushgrove does not provide an account or cross-device sync. Removing the app may remove your progress.

How do I delete my progress?

Go to Settings → Your data → Delete all progress, then confirm. This deletes the local session and island records; it does not delete emails you have sent to support.
